## Purpose

Every figure in the case study is classified below into one of three tiers. This log exists so a reader can tell, without re-tracing my research, which numbers they can rely on and which they cannot.

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Traced to a primary source: Meta's filings, investor materials, earnings transcripts, Meta engineering publications, European Commission statements, or the court's opinion |
| **REPORTED — SECONDARY** | Published by a credible third party (trade press, market research house, industry body) but not independently verifiable by me |
| **ASSUMPTION — VALIDATION REQUIRED** | My own estimate, a derived calculation, or a figure from a source with a commercial interest in the number, undisclosed methodology, or undisclosed sample |

**Critical timing note.** Meta reports Q2 2026 results on **July 29, 2026 — three days after this case study was published.** Every Meta financial and operational figure marked VERIFIED in this log is from **Q1 2026 (reported April 29, 2026)** or **full-year 2025**. Every Q2 2026 figure appearing anywhere in the case study is an analyst forecast and is labelled as such. No forecast is presented as a result.

## Known conflicts carried forward unresolved

1. **Meta's +12% price per ad vs practitioner +20% CPM.** Not reconciled. Plausibly explained by regional and objective mix, but I did not verify that. This is the highest-value open item — resolving it by vertical would materially strengthen §18.
2. **Three irreconcilable India market-size forecasts.** No blended figure produced; conflict documented instead.
3. **Meta's AI performance claims are unfalsifiable externally.** No independent replication of the GEM or Advantage+ Creative figures exists. Treated as hypotheses throughout.
4. **Q2 2026 is unknown at publication.** Reports July 29, 2026.

## Validation actions before any external or professional circulation

1. Retrieve Meta's own help-centre documentation for the January 12, 2026 attribution change and confirm every element of the before/after table in §65 Appendix C. This is the single most important verification step, because the case study's central argument rests on it.
2. Locate any Meta announcement of the March 2026 delivery change; if none exists, state explicitly that the change is inferred by practitioners from observed behaviour rather than announced.
3. Attempt to find at least one advertiser-side holdout study with disclosed methodology to replace or challenge A1.
4. Confirm the "over 4 million advertisers" generative-AI figure against a primary Meta source.
5. Replace constructed personas with three to four real practitioner interviews.
6. Re-check all figures against Meta's Q2 2026 results after July 29, 2026, and add a dated addendum rather than silently editing the original — the same versioning discipline this case study argues Meta should adopt.
